## 1. Business Profile & Structural Operating Advantages

Vista Energy, S.A.B. de C.V. is an independent exploration and production company operating in the core "sweet spot" of the Vaca Muerta shale basin in the Neuquén Basin of Argentina:
1. **Prolific Geology:** Vista's primary operated concessions—**Bajada del Palo Oeste (BPO)**, **Aguada Federal**, and the recently consolidated **La Amarga Chica (PEPASA)**—demonstrate reservoir thickness (>250 meters) and organic richness (TOC > 5%) rivaling the tier-1 core of the Delaware Basin in Texas/New Mexico.
2. **Structural Cost Leadership:** Direct lifting costs of **$4.65/boe in 2025E declining to $4.40/boe by 2029E** establish Vista as the lowest-cost producer in Latin America, generating cash-positive operating margins even if Brent drops to $30.00/bbl.
3. **Midstream Evacuation De-Bottlenecking:** Takeaway capacity constraints that historically limited Vaca Muerta are actively dissipating. The Oldelval pipeline expansion (*Proyecto Duplicar*) expands crude evacuation from 225,000 to 540,000 bbl/d, providing dedicated takeaway for Vista's volume ramp from 105,000 boe/d in 2025E to 190,000 boe/d in 2029E.
